# Monetizing ClipForge

## Own channels (faceless clips)

Pick a niche (motivation, tech explainers, true crime, history, animal facts). Post 3–5 per day. Use the hook formulas built into ClipForge. Cross-post to TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

YouTube Shorts revenue share requires original content or transformative use. Re-uploads of other people's content without commentary/captions/editing are demonetised. Prefer content you own or have licensed.

## Service business

Offer clipping for podcasters and coaches. Charge $300–$1,500/month per client for a set number of clips. Pitch with a free sample made in 10 minutes using ClipForge.

## SaaS

Host the UI. Pricing model (based on market):
- Free: 60 min/month with watermark
- Starter: $19/mo for 150 min
- Pro: $49/mo for 500 min
- Overage: $0.50/min

Cost per minute on a rented GPU (RunPod/Modal): ~$0.01–$0.03. Gross margin above 80%.

Stack: Stripe subscriptions, BullMQ + Redis job queue, GPU workers for Whisper.

## Marketplace

Sell caption style packs and hook templates. 30-day launch plan:
1. Build 3 style packs (Neon, Minimal, Retro).
2. Record 5 demo reels using free CC0 content.
3. Post daily on TikTok/Shorts showing "before and after" caption styles.
4. Offer first pack at $9, bundle at $29.
5. Partner with 5 micro-influencers for affiliate rev-share.
